Live Metronome & Timing Trainer

Click grid + live mic onset detection, compared directly against the metronome's real click times — no offline processing, no AI.

Setup

Idle — click Start to arm the metronome and request microphone access.

Microphone access is requested only when you click Start. The stream is released when you click Stop.

Use headphones. The onset detector reacts to any percussive transient it hears, including the metronome click itself if it leaks out of speakers into the mic — headphones keep the click track out of the mic's signal so only your strokes are detected.
